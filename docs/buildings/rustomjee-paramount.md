# Rustomjee Paramount

**Where:** Khar West, Mumbai. **Use:** residential.

## Building facts
- Multi-wing gated community by Rustomjee. The developer page says about 3.5 acres and architects Patty Mak and Sanjay Puri (page opened 28 Nov 2026).
- Developer page: OC received for Wings A, B, C, D and E. The final wing is marketed as the "La Mer Residences" with 2 and 3 BHK homes. It lists MahaRERA P51800000481 (Wing C) and P51800020843 (Wing F).
- The page's own copy is inconsistent on unit mix (a text block says 3 and 4 BHK, the headline says 2 and 3 BHK). Ask which wing.

## Amenities as stated by the developer
- Alfresco sky lounge, banquet hall, business centre, gymnasium, indoor games zone, mini theatre, podium, spa and salon, swimming pool, toddler's creative studio, senior citizen's zone.
- These are the developer's marketing claims from the page below, not checked on site. Do not assume an amenity exists unless listed.

## Caveats and look-alikes
- Wings are different phases with different status and RERA numbers. Ask for the wing and floor.
- Status lines are as of the source and date named; check current MahaRERA or the developer before relying on them.
- Broker posts naming this building are claims, not facts.
