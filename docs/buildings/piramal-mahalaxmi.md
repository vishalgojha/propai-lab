# Piramal Mahalaxmi

**Where:** Sane Guruji Marg, Jacob Circle, Mahalaxmi, Mumbai 400011. **Use:** residential.

## Building facts
- Piramal Realty project of three towers (South, Central, North) on about 4 acres, overlooking Mahalaxmi Racecourse. Towers range from 49 to 66 storeys (developer FAQ).
- South Tower: 2 and 3 BHK, delivered. Central Tower: 3 and 4 BHK plus penthouse collection. North Tower: 2, 3 and 4.5 BHK, topped out and under construction, per the developer page on 28 Nov 2026.

## Amenities as stated by the developer
- Podium: grand lawn, 5-lane lap pool, children's adventure meadow, jogging/bicycle track, half basketball court, amphitheatre, children's pool, cricket pitch, pool pavilion, sitting planter and garden, party lawn, meditation garden, open deck sitting area, climbing wall, jacuzzi.
- Clubhouse: multipurpose court, squash court, theatre, gymnasium, yoga, tennis, indoor games room, toddlers' room, spa, resident's lounge, banquet hall, helix party/viewing terrace.
- Tower-specific: South Tower has an exclusive fitness centre.
- The developer states 36+ amenities across about 1.4 lakh sq ft.
- These are the developer's marketing claims from the pages below, not checked on site. Do not assume an amenity exists unless listed.
