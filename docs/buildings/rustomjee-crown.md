# Rustomjee Crown

**Where:** Gokhale Road South, Prabhadevi, Mumbai 400025. **Use:** residential.

## Building facts
- Three-tower gated estate by Rustomjee, 5.75 acres per the developer. 3, 4 and 5 BHK. Two towers rise to 68 floors and one to 67 (developer FAQ).
- Phase 1 (Towers A and B): MahaRERA P51900003268, OC received. Phase 2 (Tower C): MahaRERA P51900006367, under construction (developer FAQ, 28 Nov 2026).
- Architect Hafeez Contractor; interiors PIA Interiors (developer FAQ).

## Amenities as stated by the developer
- Poolside amphitheatre, squash court, outdoor dining deck, kids' creative corner, ballroom, gym, private cinema, yoga deck.
- Outdoor zones named: Lagoon Pool, Aroma Garden, Hot Tubs, Solitude Forest, Sunken Court, Cloudwalk, English Court. Also lounge cafe, multi-play court, floating lounge, indoor games room.
- The count is stated inconsistently: the project page says "over 60 lifestyle amenities", the amenities page title says 27. Both are recorded as the developer states them.
- These are the developer's marketing claims from the pages below, not checked on site. Do not assume an amenity exists unless listed.

## Caveats and look-alikes
- "Crown" alone is ambiguous; this is Rustomjee Crown, Prabhadevi, not other buildings named Crown. Tower A, B and C are different phases with different status: ask which tower.
