# Rustomjee Seasons

**Where:** Bandra East, minutes from BKC. **Use:** residential.

## Building facts
- Developer page: 6 towers, 3 wings, about 650 units, designed by Sanjay Puri Architects, views of the Mithi River (page opened).
- Rustomjee markets 3 BHK (about 1,004 sq ft carpet) on this page.
- Rustomjee gated community of about 3.8 acres in Bandra East, minutes from BKC (developer page).

## Amenities as stated by the developer
- Swimming pool, fully-equipped gym, mini-theatre, indoor games room, banquet hall, health cafe, multipurpose court, tennis court, toddlers' creative studio, kids' play area, maze garden, jogging track, forest walk, open-to-sky podium.
- Senior citizen zone with a reflexology path, performance studio and alfresco dining area.
- These are the developer's marketing claims from the page below, not checked on site. Do not assume an amenity exists unless listed.

## Additional developer-page facts (28 Nov 2026)
- Newer developer page (rustomjee.com, opened 28 Nov 2026): 3.8-acre gated community of six wings in BKC Annexe near Kala Nagar; says it has its Occupancy Certificate and is sold out except Sky Chambers, a Wing D duplex penthouse (4,576 sq ft carpet). The older entry above says 3 wings; the sources disagree, so ask for the wing before assuming. MahaRERA P51800021028 is given for Wing D only.
