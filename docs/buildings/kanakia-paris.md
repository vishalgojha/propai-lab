# Kanakia Paris

**Where:** BKC / Bandra East. **Use:** residential.

## Building facts
- Kanakia Spaces Realty. About 1.82 acres, 7 towers of about 20 storeys, roughly 465 units, 2, 2.5 and 3 BHK; shown as completed Dec 2021 (Quikr, opened).

## Amenities as stated by the developer
- Swimming pool, steam and sauna, cafe, spa and salon, gym, squash court, terrace garden.
- Described as having a 40 ft replica of the Eiffel tower and Parisian-style landscaping.
- These are the developer's marketing claims from the page below, not checked on site. Do not assume an amenity exists unless listed.
