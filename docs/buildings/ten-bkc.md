# Ten BKC (Adani Ten BKC)

**Where:** BKC / Bandra East. **Use:** residential.

## Building facts
- Redevelopment on about 5 acres (project area about 20,149 sq m per RERA data) at MIG Colony, Bandra East, between Madhusudan Kalelkar Road (south) and N. Dharmadhikari Road (north).
- MahaRERA P51800004889. RERA data lists 15 towers/wings (Wing 1 to 15); sampled wings show 27 to 34 floors. Apartments range from 1 BHK to 5 BHK. Livemint describes 22 to 29 storeys across three zones.
- History: started by Valor Estate (formerly DB Realty) with Radius Estates; Radius defaulted on loans and faced insolvency; the committee of creditors and the NCLT approved Adani GoodHomes' bid; Valor holds a 50% economic stake (Livemint, Jan 2025). Gross development value about Rs 4,544 crore (Livemint).
- Valor (Livemint, Jan 2025) calls the project "XBKC".
- Name variants in sources: "Ten BKC", "Adani Ten BKC" (Square Yards) and "XBKC" / "X BKC" (Valor via Livemint).

## Amenities as stated by the developer
- Tiered landscape gardens, walking path, kids' play area, swimming pool and a separate kids' pool, reflexology path, senior citizens' area, clubhouse, multipurpose hall, squash courts, welfare centre, game simulator, salon and spa, convenience store, fitness centre.
- A 45,000 sq ft lobby and 2.9 acres of open spaces are claimed on the developer page.
- These are the developer's marketing claims from the page below, not checked on site. Do not assume an amenity exists unless listed.

## Additional developer-page facts (28 Nov 2026)
- Adani Realty page (opened 28 Nov 2026) gives a project area of 20,149.40 sq m (4.98 acres) and 3, 4 and 5 BHK units of 976.51 to 3,688.50 sq ft. It markets 3, 4 and 5 BHK; the RERA-based line above lists 1 to 5 BHK across wings, so check the wing.

## Caveats and look-alikes
- RERA data labels the towers Wing 1 to 15. Wing labels used informally elsewhere are not mapped to these.
- Because it spans 15 wings, "Ten BKC" alone does not identify a flat; ask for wing and floor.
- Status lines are as of the source and date named; check current MahaRERA or the developer before relying on them.
- Broker posts naming this building are claims, not facts.
